# Sustainable Fan Travel

An Aston Martin F1 fan experience connecting sustainable travel with estimated carbon impact and fan engagement.

## Language

**Journey**:
A fan's trip from a starting location to a destination.

**Race journey**:
A journey to a selected race, or return travel from it, when the fan is already in the host country or location.

**Race destination**:
The arrival location used to plan travel to the selected race. The appropriate circuit entrance or arrival point remains to be agreed.

**Route option**:
A possible way to complete a journey, compared with alternatives using practical information and estimated emissions.

**Race weekend**:
The event-specific reward window from Thursday 00:00 through Monday 06:00 in the race's local timezone. A rewarded journey must both start and complete inside this window.

**Daily journey allowance**:
One rewarded outbound journey and one rewarded return journey per race-local calendar day. Each journey retains its own 2,000-point cap. Day attribution for journeys crossing midnight remains to be specified.

**Estimated emissions**:
An estimate of a journey's carbon impact, rather than a direct measurement of emissions.
_Avoid_: Measured emissions

**Estimated emissions reduction**:
The estimated difference between a journey's emissions and a comparison baseline. The current proposed baseline is one person driving between the same endpoints, pending final confirmation.
_Avoid_: Verified carbon savings, carbon offsets

**Journey validation**:
Assessment of a fan's reported transport mode against the recorded route, distance, and duration. This plausibility assessment does not establish the transport mode used.
_Avoid_: Proof of bus use

**Points**:
Recognition earned at 50 points per estimated kg CO₂e reduced on eligible journeys, capped at 2,000 points per journey. The calculation's dataset and comparison baseline still require final confirmation.

**Provisional journey points**:
The estimated award for an unfinished journey, adjusted when its assessed impact changes. These points enter the earned balance only on qualifying completion; reductions do not remove previously earned points.

**Fan rewards**:
Benefits fans can use points toward, including store discounts, driver/team challenges, and individual tree redemptions. Availability and fulfilment arrangements are not established.

**Fan challenge**:
A proposed activity for drivers or the team that costs 500 points to submit and requires approval before fans can contribute. Contributed points act as votes; approved unfinished challenges remain in the backlog without refunds.

**Aston Martin admin**:
A user authorized to approve fan challenges, including users identified by an Aston Martin admin badge. The badge indicates an assigned role rather than a cosmetic fan reward.

**Challenge contribution**:
An amount of at least 10 earned points spent to increase an approved challenge's ranking, limited by the fan's available balance. Contributions are not refunded when the challenge carries into the backlog.
_Avoid_: Cash funding

**Challenge submission fee**:
The non-refundable 500 earned points spent to submit a new fan challenge for admin approval. This fee is not returned if the submission is rejected and does not count toward the challenge's ranking. Editing and resubmission rules remain to be agreed.

**Challenge backlog**:
Approved challenges awaiting performance, including those not selected for the current fan interaction. Their contributed point totals and histories are retained without refunds.

**Rewards store**:
A place to redeem accumulated points for merchandise discounts ranging from 10% to 60%, each priced in points by an admin. Larger discounts are intended to require substantially more points.

**Fan interaction session**:
A scheduled stage appearance by the drivers to interact with fans. The top three approved challenges by contributed points are selected before that appearance; precise voting cutoffs remain to be agreed.

**Tree redemption**:
An individual tree-planting request bought with earned points at an admin-set price and assigned to the purchasing account. Redemption does not by itself confirm a planted tree or a quantity of carbon removed.

**Tree dedication**:
The purchasing account's name associated with its redeemed tree. An optional quote is proposed; visibility remains to be agreed.

**Planting reassignment**:
Moving an unfulfilled tree-planting request to a project in another country when its original project cannot deliver. It is not relocation of an already-planted tree.
