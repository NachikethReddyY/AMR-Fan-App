# Sustainable Fan Travel

An Aston Martin F1 fan experience connecting sustainable travel with estimated carbon impact and fan engagement.

## Language

**Journey**:
A fan's trip from a starting location to a destination.

**Race journey**:
A journey to a selected race, or return travel from it, when the fan is already in the host country or location.

**Race destination**:
The arrival location used to plan travel to the selected race. The appropriate circuit entrance or arrival point remains to be agreed.

**Route option**:
A possible way to complete a journey, compared with alternatives using practical information and estimated emissions.

**Race weekend**:
The event-specific reward window from Thursday 00:00 through Monday 06:00 in the race's local timezone. Eligibility for journeys crossing its boundaries remains to be agreed.

**Estimated emissions**:
An estimate of a journey's carbon impact, rather than a direct measurement of emissions.
_Avoid_: Measured emissions

**Estimated emissions reduction**:
The estimated difference between a journey's emissions and a comparison baseline. The baseline has not yet been agreed.
_Avoid_: Verified carbon savings, carbon offsets

**Journey validation**:
Assessment of evidence about a recorded journey. Route, distance, and duration consistency alone does not establish the transport mode used.
_Avoid_: Proof of bus use

**Points**:
Recognition for sustainable travel, with lower-carbon route choices intended to earn more. The calculation, comparison basis, and eligibility rules remain to be agreed.

**Fan rewards**:
Benefits fans can use points toward, including store discounts and driver/team challenges. Availability and fulfilment arrangements are not established.

**Fan challenge**:
A proposed activity for drivers or the team that requires approval before fans can contribute earned points. Approval, funding, and fulfilment are distinct; contribution rules remain unresolved.

**Aston Martin admin**:
A user authorized to approve fan challenges, including users identified by an Aston Martin admin badge. The badge indicates an assigned role rather than a cosmetic fan reward.

**Challenge contribution**:
Earned points committed by a fan toward a fan challenge. Deduction timing and refund rules remain unresolved.
_Avoid_: Cash funding

**Rewards store**:
A place to redeem accumulated points for merchandise discounts ranging from 10% to 60%. Larger discounts require substantially more points; exact costs and discount terms remain unresolved.
