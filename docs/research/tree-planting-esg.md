# Tree planting: official ESG basis

Checked 25 September 2026. Scope: two official Make A Mark reports, located through the [team's Make A Mark page](https://www.astonmartinf1.com/en-GB/make-a-mark). Page numbers below are both printed numbers and one-based PDF pages; they coincide. Text extraction and visual inspection covered the cited pages.

## Evidence status

| Dimension | Status and sample | Counting method and evidence | Confidence and limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Verified reported facts

- **Campus planting:** The 2024 report records 1,500 trees of UK native origin and grown in the UK, planted at the AMR Technology Campus. It describes woodland and hedgerow planting as habitat measures. This is physical planting evidence, separate from the later removal programme. [2024 report, printed/PDF pp. 35-36](https://downloads.astonmartinf1.com/MakeAMark_ESG_Report_2024.pdf#page=35).
- **Ethiopia:** Community woodland restoration and agroforestry in the North Ethiopian Highlands. The report describes more than eight million trees and annual sequestration exceeding 97,000 tCO₂e at project level; these are not AMR's attributable totals. Carbon-credit revenue supports local priorities. [2025 report, printed/PDF p. 26](https://downloads.astonmartinf1.com/MakeAMark_ESG_Report_2025.pdf#page=26).
- **Kenya:** Mangrove restoration at Mtwapa Creek, near Mombasa, includes growing native mangroves from seedlings. [2025 report, printed/PDF p. 26](https://downloads.astonmartinf1.com/MakeAMark_ESG_Report_2025.pdf#page=26).
- **Purchases and accounting:** Footnote 3 identifies Climate Impact Partners as the purchase channel and quality-assurance provider. The reported 2,124 tCO₂e removed in 2025 combines woodland, mangroves and US concrete mineralisation. It cannot be attributed to trees alone or converted into an AMR tree count. [2025 report, printed/PDF p. 91, footnote 3](https://downloads.astonmartinf1.com/MakeAMark_ESG_Report_2025.pdf#page=91).

## Uncertainty

The cited 2025 sections do not identify local project operators, a per-project AMR allocation, credit-retirement records, a per-tree price, or a fan-redemption arrangement. They establish purchase-backed carbon-removal support, not that AMR employees or fans planted the restoration projects' trees. [2025 report, printed/PDF pp. 26 and 91](https://downloads.astonmartinf1.com/MakeAMark_ESG_Report_2025.pdf#page=26).

## Possible app linkage (proposal, not reported fact)

A tree-planting reward has a credible thematic connection to the team's published biodiversity work. In a proof of concept, treat it as simulated redemption with clearly labelled demo progress. A real points-to-planting service would need an agreed fulfilment partner, funding and conversion rules, and evidence linking each redemption to delivered planting or restoration. Do not imply that Climate Impact Partners or Aston Martin has agreed to that arrangement, and do not use the team's carbon-removal total as app impact.
