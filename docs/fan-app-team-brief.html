Aston Martin fan app — team brief

Help fans compare lower-carbon ways to reach a race, reward completed journeys, and turn earned points into participation with the team.

This brief brings together the product interview and supplied meeting summary. Agreed rules are distinguished from proposals and open decisions. It describes the intended core app; it does not report a finished implementation.

01 / Product direction

Start with the journey to the race

The fan is already in the host location. They choose a race from the F1 calendar, compare routes from their current location, travel, and earn points. The return journey is included. The first feature sits within a broader fan app with challenges, a rewards store, and tree redemptions.

  1. Select the race

    Use the current location and the race’s arrival point. Planning ahead is allowed.

  2. Compare the routes

    Put estimated emissions beside journey time and estimated points. Highlight the lowest emissions among the available options.

  3. Choose and travel

    Start actual location tracking and continue through arrival, including while the phone is locked.

  4. Assess the journey

    Check the reported mode, route, distance, duration, arrival, and reward eligibility.

  5. Receive the points

    Show the final estimated reduction and credit the qualifying award once.

  6. Choose a fan payoff

    Submit or support challenges, redeem a merchandise discount, or buy an individual tree with points.

Current scope: local travel to and from a selected race. Everyday destinations remain a broader direction from the earlier meeting brief; international flights are outside this first journey. Country examples do not establish actual scheduled races.

03 / Agreed rules

Where fans spend their points

Driver and team challenges

Fans propose activities and use points to support the approved ideas they want to see performed. An example from the interview was a driver doing a chicken dance before a Singapore fan interaction.

  1. Submit a challenge · 500 points

    Deduct the submission fee from the fan’s available balance. The 500 points are non-refundable, even if the challenge is rejected. They never count toward ranking.

  2. Get admin approval

    Only an admin, including someone assigned the Aston Martin admin badge, can approve. Unapproved or rejected challenges cannot receive contributions. The badge represents permission granted by the system.

  3. Contribute points · minimum 10

    A fan can contribute any amount of at least 10, up to their available balance. Contributions are spent points and count toward the challenge’s ranking.

  4. Select the top three

    Select the three approved challenges with the highest contribution totals before the drivers’ fan-interaction stage appearance.

    Open question

    The precise cutoff and tie rules remain open.

  5. Record performance, or keep the backlog

    Approval, selection, and performance are separate states. Approved challenges not yet performed retain their points and history in the backlog for later fulfilment. No challenge refunds.

Open question

Rejected content is not a promise of a future performance. Editing and resubmission rules, including whether another fee applies, are still open. A proposal or ranking does not establish driver or team agreement to perform it.

Merchandise discounts

  • Redeem points for 10%–60% discounts.
  • Admins set each reward’s points price. Larger discounts should be substantially harder to earn.
  • Show the points cost and offer terms before redemption.
  • Use clearly labelled sample vouchers in the POC.
    For discussion

    Prices, merchant supply, and commercial terms need team input.

Individual trees

  • Buy individual trees with earned points at an admin-set points price per tree.
  • Assign every tree to the purchasing account and that account’s name.
  • If the original country cannot fulfil it, move the pending planting request to another country.
  • Open question

    An optional quote remains a proposal. Visibility, notifications, and the no-alternative fallback remain open.

Planting is a disclosed demonstration for the POC

Redeeming points creates a request, not evidence that a tree has been planted. A real service needs funding, a planting partner, and fulfilment evidence. Reassignment moves an unfulfilled request, not an already-planted tree. Keep tree activity separate from estimated travel emissions; do not claim immediate carbon removal.

Decisions still open

The direction and numeric rules above are recorded. The items below are carried forward for discussion, with suggestions clearly labelled. The interview is not being declared fully closed.

  1. Carbon baseline and fair comparisons

    Open question

    Confirm the one-person driving baseline, whole-point rounding, the emissions boundary, and the factors for each supported location and mode. Decide how to disclose any proxy data.

    For discussion

    Proposal: use one consistent per-passenger CO₂e boundary, source/version every factor, and show the estimate basis. Avoid implying that Google supplies a complete multimodal carbon calculation.

  2. Journey boundaries and evidence

    Open question

    Choose race entrances, the return destination rule, arrival detection, acceptable tracking gaps, and what fans see when automatic validation fails. Set which race-local day owns a journey crossing midnight and the exact Monday cutoff behavior.

    For discussion

    Proposal: attribute an overnight journey to its start day; require arrival before Monday 06:00. Neither detail is agreed yet. The already accepted daily limit remains one outbound and one return.

  3. Challenge moderation and stage selection

    Open question

    Can fans edit and resubmit rejected ideas, and does that cost another submission fee? How are ties, fewer than three eligible ideas, zero-contribution ideas, postponed stage appearances, and backlog entry into later selections handled?

    Fixed rule: the original 500-point fee is never returned and never counts toward ranking.

    Open question

    Free resubmission has not been agreed. The exact vote-close time must precede the relevant stage appearance.

    Open question
